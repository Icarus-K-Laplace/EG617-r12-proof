# R(4,48) floor 310 via q20 rotation

Status: `PROVED`.

## Theorem

Every `C in R(4,48)` satisfies

`e(C) >= 310`.

This strengthens the previous 309-edge floor.

## Proof

The audited 48-point argument gives `delta(C)>=12`. If `delta(C)>=13`, handshake gives `e(C)>=312`, so only a degree-12 pivot can matter.

Assume for contradiction `e(C)=309`. Take a degree-12 pivot `p`, with 12-shell `A` and 35-core `H`. Then

`309 = 78 + q + e(H)`.

The shell floor is `q>=20`, while clean CORE211 gives `e(H)>=211`. Therefore

`q=20`, `e(H)=211`.

The q20 equality classification gives

`M = complement(C[A]) = K_{2,r} disjoint_union (10-r)K1`, `6<=r<=10`.

Let the two centres be `x1,x2`. Each leaf `y` has a 2-vertex core row `P_y`, each isolate has empty core row, and each centre has a 10-vertex core row. The leaf rows are disjoint from both centre rows. Every leaf and isolate has degree exactly 12 in `C`.

Fix a leaf `y` and write `P_y={u,w}`. Repivot at `y`. Since `y` also has degree 12 and `e(C)=309`, the same floor argument forces the new decomposition again to satisfy

`q_y=20`, `e(H_y)=211`,

so the new shell missing graph `M_y` is again `K_{2,r_y}` plus isolates with `6<=r_y<=10`.

The new shell consists of

`{p} union (A \ {y,x1,x2}) union {u,w}`.

Because `p` is anticomplete to the old core `H`, both `pu` and `pw` are missing target edges, hence edges of `M_y`. Since the q20 missing graph is triangle-free, `uw` must be a target edge. Thus `p` has missing degree exactly 2 in `M_y`. Since the two centres of a `K_{2,r_y}` have missing degree at least 6, `p` is a leaf and `u,w` are the two centres.

Now compare `P_y` with every other old leaf row. In the new `K_{2,r_y}` normal form, any old shell vertex is either adjacent in `M_y` to both centres `u,w` or to neither. Hence every other old leaf row is either exactly `P_y` or disjoint from `P_y`; old isolates are disjoint as well.

Let `c` be the number of old leaves whose row equals `P_y`, including `y`. The leaves of the new `K_{2,r_y}` are exactly

- `p`,
- the `r-c` old leaves whose rows are disjoint from `P_y`,
- the `10-r` old isolates.

Therefore

`r_y = 1 + (r-c) + (10-r) = 11-c`.

In the q20 classification, each centre has decoration `10-r_y=c-1`. Thus

`d_C(u)=12+(c-1)=11+c`.

On the other hand, in the original pivot decomposition, `u` is adjacent to exactly the `c` old shell leaves in the row-class of `P_y`: it is not adjacent to `p`, to the two old centres (their 10-rows are disjoint from `P_y`), to old isolates, or to leaves from other row classes. Hence

`d_C(u)=d_H(u)+c`.

Comparing gives

`d_H(u)=11`,

contradicting `delta(H)>=12` for `H in R(3,35)`.

Therefore `e(C)=309` is impossible, and `e(C)>=310`.
