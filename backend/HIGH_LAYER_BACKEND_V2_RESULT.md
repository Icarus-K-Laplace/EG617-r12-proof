The package reports a complete exact system for adjacent legal degree-13 pivots. In the notation of the package,

`A_y={p} union T union D`,

`H_y=(H\D) union S`,

## q30-q33 local obstruction to one-step Phi monotonicity

The package constructs four exact local two-pivot certificates for q30, q31, q32 and q33 showing that

`active non-twin pivot => Phi strictly increases in one step`

is false as a purely local statement.

Therefore the next valid target must use at least one of:

- a third pivot;
- joint constraints between distinct S-rows;
- a nonadjacent-pivot weighted inequality;
- a stronger global compatibility condition.
