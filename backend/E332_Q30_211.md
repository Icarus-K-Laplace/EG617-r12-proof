## Equality channels d=3 and d=10

For `d=3` or `d=10`, `d(13-d)=30`; therefore every nonnegative slack term in the exact repivot formula must vanish.

Consequences include:

- complementary missing subgraph has zero edges;
- complementary shell decoration is zero;
- the core row `D` is a clique of degree-12 core vertices;
- shell-to-row cross target edges vanish in the equality position.

These equality constraints are the active route to a multi-pivot row-geometry contradiction.
