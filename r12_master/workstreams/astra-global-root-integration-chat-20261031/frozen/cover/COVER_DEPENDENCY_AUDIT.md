## Corrections and proof gaps found in the historical presentation

1. The old E405 high/low-cut paragraph switches from “at most one high neighbor” to the claim that a vertex of excess at least2 has no regular neighbor. That implication requires the stronger assumption G_p<=1 at every regular p, not merely an unweighted neighbor count. The intended contradiction can begin with G_p<=1; the new proof avoids this cut argument entirely by excluding all q<=26 shells directly.
2. Some historical odd-cycle steps say only that a nonbipartite matching3 shell is too sparse. Section4 supplies all C5/C7 cases, including exterior edges and attachments, with explicit bounds21,14,10,7.
3. The global repository's CORE211 headline does not itself prove its degree12 input; its completeness audit explicitly identifies that old gap. The later783c720 source supplies the missing cap argument. Section8 then gives the exact AES application, so no unproved historical seed is silently retained.
4. A <=286 minimum-degree gate cannot be used at287/288 merely by analogy. Section8 proves the extension separately before q29/q30 use.
5. For inserted vertices the necessary inequality is t(S)-2e_M(S)>=d(beta+1-d), not the weaker expression with just one internal-edge subtraction. This exact coefficient is used in the triangle and K4 boundary cases and checked against actual graph exchanges.
6. A closed repivot walk balances signed corrections; it does not make them all zero. No connectivity or cycle argument is used. Corrections vanish only when (3) meets an independently imposed maximum charge with equality.

These are explicit repairs or bypasses; no new graph counterexample to the final historical COVER statement is claimed.
