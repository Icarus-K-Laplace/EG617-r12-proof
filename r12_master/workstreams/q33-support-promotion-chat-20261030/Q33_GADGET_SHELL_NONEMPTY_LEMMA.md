# Q33 nonempty gadget-shell lemma

Status: PROVED_CONDITIONAL under the registered exact-129 factorization and shell partition.

After choosing seven isolated K5 components avoiding S, seven shell vertices remain. The three unselected K5 components can contain at most three B vertices and at most the three S vertices. The pivot p cannot share a non-target K5 with any point of A=S union B. Hence these three components can contain at most six remaining shell vertices, forcing at least one shell vertex into the 11-vertex gadget.

The gadget core has at most ten vertices, so for the three unselected component core sizes:
\[
g_C=19-\sum_{i=1}^3c_i,\quad 7\le g_C\le10,\quad 9\le\sum_i c_i\le12.
\]
This shell-only bound is superseded by Q33_KP_GADGET_CORE_PROFILE_REFINEMENT.md, which uses the two K5 parts plus the exceptional vertex to improve the lower endpoint to10.
