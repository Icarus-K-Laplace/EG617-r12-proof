## Lemma: universal charge ceiling

For every pivot in a legal E-edge residual,

q_p = E-91-e(H_p) <= E-305.

Thus the ceiling is q<=28 when E=333 and q<=29 when E=334. The same ceiling applies to every repivot in the same residual, because the universal core floor applies to every degree-13 pivot.

## E333

Assume a legal residual F has E=333. Every degree-13 pivot has q<=28.

The registered q27 closure excludes q=27. Therefore any pivot must have q=28. But the q28 repivot theorem produces another degree-13 pivot with q' >=30, while the universal ceiling gives q'<=28. Contradiction.

Hence no legal E333 residual exists, relative to the registered q27 shell dependencies.

## E334

Assume a legal residual F has E=334. Every degree-13 pivot has q<=29.

The q27 closure excludes q=27. If a pivot has q=28, the q28 repivot theorem gives q'>=30, contradicting q'<=29. If a pivot has q=29, the q29 good-repivot theorem gives q'>=30, again contradicting q'<=29.

Every possible pivot charge is excluded. Hence no legal E334 residual exists, relative to the registered q27 shell dependencies.
