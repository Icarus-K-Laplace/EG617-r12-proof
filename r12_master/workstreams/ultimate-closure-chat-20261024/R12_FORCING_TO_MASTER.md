# R12 forcing-to-master bridge audit

## Target theorem

For every legal maximal-pivot shell survivor S arising from the original balanced K145 context, prove an exhaustive alternative:

1. S yields three independent degree-12 vertices in a legal 35-core, so e(H)>=214 and q<=E-305;
2. S yields a common-S owner subsystem satisfying the exact row-closure contract of GLOBAL_SUPPORT_COMPATIBILITY_MASTER_THEOREM.md, then enters boundary one-loss / low-cross support machinery;
3. S reduces by a proved loss descent to an explicitly finite shallow-loss residue, with all equality cases accounted for.

## What the source theorem stack proves

- Zero-charge incidence lemma: if w_u=w_v=0 and uv is a target edge, the support signatures are equal; hence distinct signatures are independent.
- In alpha(H)<=3, at most three zero-charge signatures occur. Three distinct signatures yield three independent degree-12 anchors.
- Under at most two signatures and boundary one-loss, every support has a nonempty zero skeleton and the family lies in at most three sunflower patterns.
- The d3 repeated patterns are Type H / Type P; hereditary packing caps owner multiplicity at the stated thresholds.
- First-high-slack loss decomposition controls plateau, one-loss, and deep-loss rows.
- d4/d9 fully decorated repeated families at q<=38 descend to d<=4 except the documented (d_M,g)=(5,1) equality escape, subsequently claimed closed by the common-S cross-budget synthesis.

## Exact gap

None of the above proves that every historical shell survivor already supplies a common-S family with the necessary row closure. GLOBAL_FORCING_TO_MASTER_ALLOWANCE_COVERAGE.md explicitly disclaims that universal extraction. The zero-signature theorem cannot be applied before this contract is derived. Nor may the one-loss low-cross hypothesis be inferred from the master theorem itself.

Thus Bridge A is still OPEN; no E335–E340 layer can be marked empty solely from the master theorem.

Use the maximal-q pivot and lexicographic tau criterion only where their hypotheses are stated. Partition the 13 shell vertices by exact missing-neighbourhood/support signature and retain the exact row/cross-edge ledger. Then prove a trichotomy for each resulting class:
- a three-anchor witness;

The first audit deliverable is the complete implication from the legal shell equations to this trichotomy. Until every shell signature profile is covered, the global bridge is not proved.
