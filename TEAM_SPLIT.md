# Three-line research split

To avoid duplicated work, the current project is partitioned into three independent lines.

## Line A — Astra A: front-end bridge

Owns:

`K145 -> legitimate R(4,49) residual`.

Primary objects: outer forcing, seven/eight genuine K12 blocks, O61, S27/S30/S31, and the final residual contract.

Must not assume the obsolete exact target `e(F)=325`.

## Line B — Astra B: high-layer backend

Owns:

`E333 ... E340` for `F in R(4,49)`.

Preferred output is a uniform repivot/core-excess stability theorem that removes several layers simultaneously.

Must not duplicate a full E332 classification unless a genuinely more general theorem automatically covers it.

## Line C — central line: E332

Owns:

`E332 -> (q,e(H))=(30,211)` and complete elimination of the q30 equality system.

Primary objects: zero-defect d=3/d=10 roots, row equal/disjoint geometry, and the two equality-core degree multisets `14,12^34` and `13^2,12^33`.

## Merge protocol

Every incoming result must provide:

1. exact hypotheses;
2. proof or reproducible certificate;
3. dependencies;
4. one status among `PROVED`, `COMPUTATION_CERTIFIED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `OPEN`, `RETRACTED`;
5. explicit statement of what remains open.

No line may declare the full EG617 r=12 theorem solved independently. Final certification requires both the complete front-end bridge and the complete R(4,49) backend.
