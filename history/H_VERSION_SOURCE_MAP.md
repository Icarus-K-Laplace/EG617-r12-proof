# H-version source map

Status: historical source map only. No `PROVED` label from H is imported automatically into the canonical proof DAG.

## Main themes recovered from H

### H247--287
- r=12 contextual/bootstrap audit.
- Derives a six-integer contextual gap for the full shell and isolates the first failing outer row near `E=224, q<=27` in the historical architecture.
- Useful for understanding why scalar edge-floor improvements alone were insufficient.

### H288--328
- Establishes an audited r=12 cap-three lower floor `B_12(3,35) >= 209` under the historical public dependencies.
- Explicitly retracts/weakens an earlier 213 claim and identifies the 209-edge equality-core problem as the remaining object.
- Also contains quotient/cut-interface compression results unrelated to the current backend.

### H329--410
- Recomputes the r=12 outer recurrence barrier.
- At the terminal `(d,j)=(11,7)` row: outer budget 342 versus hereditary residual ceiling 340, while strict recurrence closure would require 343.
- General recomputation shows the same obstruction across terminal degree rows `d=4,...,11`, with the strongest scalar gap near `d=7,8`.
- Important correction: no nonempty 49-vertex residual can fix this by a stronger scalar edge lower bound alone; one needs emptiness/structural exclusion or a different outer architecture.

### H411--533
- Large weighted-quotient / endpoint-capacity classification program for historical `e(F)=325` candidates.
- Many statements are finite computations and are not transported into the modern proof DAG without replay.

### H534--574
- Uses the historical lower bound `B_12(3,35) >= 209` to show that certain alpha-excess-2 shell pivots force a 209-edge extremal 35-core.
- This is a rigidity program around overlapping 209-edge cores, not the modern CORE211 theorem.
- H itself notes that the 209 lower-bound certificate was not independently rebuilt from first principles in that run.

### H575--606
- Develops codegree / common-neighbour rigidity for a 35-vertex, 387-edge complement-side object.
- Potentially useful as a structural lemma source, but not directly part of the current canonical 49-vertex backend.

## Canonical interpretation

H is valuable mainly as:
1. a correction of the outer-bootstrap architecture;
2. a source for the audited 209-core floor and equality-core program;
3. a source of weighted quotient and codegree rigidity ideas.

H does **not** by itself supply the modern clean `CORE211` proof and must not be cited as if it did.
