## Canonicalization rule

Raw source bundles are NOT the proof.

A result enters the canonical DAG only after its exact assumptions and proof route are recorded in one of:

- `ASSET_LEDGER.md`
- `CANONICAL_STATE.md`
- `PROOF_DAG.md`
- `front_end/*`
- `backend/*`
- `audits/*`

## Binary archival note

The historical PDF/ZIP sources are hundreds of megabytes and are intentionally not duplicated into the GitHub proof repository through the text-oriented connector. The repository stores the canonical mathematical reconstruction, certificates/scripts that are small and auditable, and a provenance index back to the supplied source bundles.
