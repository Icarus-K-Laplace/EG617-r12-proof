# E406 d=3 pure-support class rigidity

Status: `PROVED` under the d=3 q31 platform contract. This does not yet close d=3 or E406.

Let y be a q31 pivot in one of the seven d=3 platforms of `E406_D3_D10_FINAL_RISE_NORMAL_FORM.md`. Let B be the 10-point clique side of its shell and D the 3-point missing-neighbour cover. Every active regular b in B has a 3-point core support

`U_b=N_C(b)`.

A support U is called **pure** when repivoting its shell vertex drops q from31 to30. By `E406_Q31_PLATFORM_SUPPORT_RIGIDITY.md`, this is equivalent to

- `C[U]=K3`;
- every vertex of U has core degree12;
- `e_F(D,U)=0`.

At least one pure support exists: the reverse edge of the q30 -> q31 final rise.

## 1. Every support is equal to or disjoint from a fixed pure support

Fix a pure support U and repivot at a shell vertex b with `U_b=U`. The new pivot has q=30 and a 285-edge core

`C_b=(C\U) union D`,

with minimum degree at least12.

Let c be any other active shell vertex, with support V=U_c and `r=|U intersect V|`.

Because c is nonadjacent to every vertex of D, its new core row after the repivot is exactly

`V\U`,

of size `3-r`.

Equivalently, in the new q30 shell its row weight is `3-r`.

Now take a vertex u in U\V. The imported shell vertex u has row weight10 in the q30 child: it had core degree12, two neighbours inside the old K3 U, and no neighbours in D, so exactly ten neighbours survive in the new core.

If `0<r<3`, then c is still missing-adjacent to u in the new shell and the two endpoint row sizes are

`10` and `3-r <=2`.

But the near-284 PAIR-13 lemma applies to the q30 285-edge core and requires row-union size at least13, hence in particular row-size sum at least13. Contradiction.

Therefore

`U intersect V` is either empty or all of U.

Since both supports have size3:

`V=U` or `U intersect V=empty`.   (CLASS-DISJOINTNESS)

This conclusion holds for **every** other active support V, including P3 plateau supports and defective K3 plateau supports; no zero-slack assumption on V is needed.

## 2. Multiplicity of a pure support is at most3

Let m be the number of active B-vertices whose support equals the same pure K3 U.

The 14-set

`{y} union B union U`

contains:

- the K11 on `{y} union B`: 55 edges;
- the K3 U: 3 edges;
- at least `3m` B-to-U support edges.

Thus it has at least

`58+3m`

edges. The hereditary cap `D(14)=69` gives

`m<=3`.   (PURE-MULTIPLICITY)

## 3. Exact q30 child generated by a pure support class

Because every other active support is either equal to U or disjoint from U, the q30 child obtained by repivoting a member of the pure class has an exact shell form.

Let the pure class have multiplicity m. Its three imported vertices each occur in exactly the m equal support rows and in no D-row. Therefore each imported shell decoration equals

`m-1`.

Among the 10 vertices on the opposite shell side:

- the other `m-1` members of the same support class are F-complete to U and hence become M-isolates;
- all remaining `11-m` vertices are F-anticomplete to U and have missing degree3.

Hence the q30 child has

`M = K_{3,11-m} + (m-1)K1`,

with

`g=(m-1,m-1,m-1)`

on the 3-side, where

`m in {1,2,3}`.

The charge is identically

`z+G = 3(11-m)+3(m-1)=30`.

Thus every pure q30 child lies in one of only three exact K3,* plateau cells.

## 4. Zero-slack support classes

A P3 plateau support consists of three core-degree12 vertices and is anticomplete to D. The same minimum-degree comparison after repivoting a pure class shows that a P3 support disjoint from the pure anchor cannot have core edges into the pure K3 anchor: otherwise one of its degree12 vertices loses an anchor neighbour and gains no D-neighbour, dropping below degree12 in the q30 core.

The same holds for another pure K3 support.

Hence distinct zero-slack support classes (P3 plateau or pure K3 child) are disjoint and pairwise anticomplete.

Since `alpha(C)<=4`, if there are p distinct P3 classes and k distinct pure-K3 classes then

`2p+k <=4`.   (ALPHA-BUDGET)

This sharply limits the number of zero-slack support classes available among the 7--10 active degree3 rows.

## 5. Current d=3 frontier

The remaining d=3 problem is now a support-class problem rather than a shell-topology problem:

- active row count is 7--10;
- at least one class is pure K3;
- every support is equal to or disjoint from that pure class;
- each pure class has multiplicity at most3;
- zero-slack classes satisfy `2p+k<=4`;
- non-zero-slack plateau supports are only defective K3s with one unit of core-excess/cross defect.

The next step is to control these defective K3 classes using the total q31 core excess4 and the PAIR-13/TRI-24 row-location restrictions.
